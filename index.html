Meet The Appellate
Project Community

Our mission is to ensure our highest courts reflect our communities. That’s why we are empowering the next generation of appellate lawyers and judges.

Many law students of color, particularly those who are first generation, lack the networks and resources of their law school peers. As a result, these students may miss out on an appellate career simply because they do not know how to pursue it. Others self-select out, discouraged by the absence of appellate attorneys and judges who share their backgrounds.

Our goal is to change that.

Our Mentorship Program is the first and only national effort focused on empowering law students of color to pursue appellate work. We pair law students interested in appellate practice with two mentors in the appellate field, ranging from attorneys, judges, professors, and law clerks. We also provide students with innovative and impactful appellate-focused resources, including clerkship support, skillbuilding opportunities, networking, and community building.

Since launching in September 2020, we are proud to have provided innovative appellate resources to 619 law students through our Mentorship Program, representing 108 law schools nationwide. Our incredible network of thousands of volunteers—appellate leaders from all over the country—tirelessly support our students and the work we do.
